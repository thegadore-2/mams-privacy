# Privacy Policy for MAMS Attendance Monitoring

**Effective Date:** July 9, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 9, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how the MAMS Attendance Monitoring application ("MAMS", "the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information. MAMS is a proprietary, business-tools application issued by an employer or partnering organization to its employees and workers for workforce attendance tracking. It is an internal operational tool and is not intended for general public use.

## 2. Information We Collect and Why
To fulfill its core function as a precise attendance and field-presence system, MAMS collects the minimum information necessary:

* **Camera Access and Photos:** We require front-facing camera access to capture a real-time photograph when you record attendance (punch in/out). This serves as required visual proof of physical presence at your assigned location. 
  * **Face Data Notice:** The app captures a standard photographic record only. It does not perform facial recognition, nor does it collect, process, or store biometric templates or unique facial identifiers. Each photo is securely uploaded and not used for any other purpose.
* **Location Data (Foreground and Background):** We collect your GPS location to verify you are within your assigned work area when punching in and out. The app also periodically checks your location in the background *only during your active shift* to confirm continued field presence. Background tracking automatically ceases when your shift ends or you punch out. It is not used to track personal movement outside of work hours.
* **Identifiers & Device Info:** Employee ID, name, assigned area, and basic device information (model, OS) are collected to authenticate the user, map attendance records, and resolve technical support issues.

## 3. Data Storage & Transmission
* Attendance data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted format when offline, and is synchronized to the employer's secure backend servers when an internet connection is established.
* All data is transmitted securely over HTTPS connections.
* The employing organization is the Data Controller for all records collected through MAMS. The developer acts solely as a Data Processor on behalf of the employer.

## 4. Data Sharing
We do not use the information for advertising, marketing, or profiling. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third-party services. Your data is accessible only to authorized administrative and HR personnel within the deploying organization.

## 5. Data Retention and Employee Rights
Attendance records and photos are retained by the employer in accordance with its own record-retention policy and applicable labor regulations. Local device data is automatically purged when an employee logs out or uninstalls the app. Employees may request access, correction, or deletion of their data (including photos) through their employer's Human Resources department.

## 6. Contact Information
For questions regarding your attendance data or workplace policy, please contact your employer's HR department.
